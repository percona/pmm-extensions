1938 :data:`_CAPTURE_HOLD_RELEASE_INTERVAL_SECONDS` to wait out the hold's poststop

1940 ever spent there: a hold already running is signalled on the first read, 

1941 and one past signalling is abandoned on it. A hold still not running 

1942 when the attempts run out keeps the allocation until its own deadline, 

1943 which is the bound the design accepts. 

1944

1946 persisted by the time this runs, and the step self-expires at its
