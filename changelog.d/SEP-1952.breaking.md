An entity-style AppSchema (non-empty entities) that declares a root-level list_view, detail_view, or task_type now fails at app import with a validation error naming each offending key, instead of being silently accepted; move list_view onto the relevant entity and remove detail_view and task_type, which entity-style schemas do not support. In-tree apps are unaffected — none builds an entity-style schema — but out-of-tree apps relying on the old behaviour must update before upgrading.
