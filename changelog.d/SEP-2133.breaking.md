Removed `POST /transform/` and `POST /history/` from the tasks API. A client still calling either now gets `405 Method Not Allowed`; for `/transform/` it arrives after a `307` redirect to `/transform`, so a client that does not follow redirects sees the `307` instead. Task-history rows are recorded only by task dispatch, and `GET /history/` and the other `/history/...` routes are unchanged.
