A script, `scripts/migrate_standalone_schedules.py`, recreates the MySQL backup schedules of a standalone v0.13.1 instance in PMM Extensions, and `docs/how-to/migrate-from-standalone-sep.md` gives the steps. Every schedule arrives paused, and nothing is created for a service that the match file does not match. Backup history, settings, secrets and users do not move; a backup taken before the migration is restored by typing its location in the Restore form.
