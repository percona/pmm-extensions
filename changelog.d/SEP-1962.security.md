A diagnostics delivery plan resolution step or upload step whose `path` carried a scheme or host, such as `https://other-host/x` or `//other-host/x`, sent the request and the credentials in its headers to that host instead of the plan's `endpoint`. Such a path is now refused when the plan is parsed, as it already was for the probe, case-search and connection-details steps. Every step kind also refuses a path whose `.` or `..` segments resolve it to another host, such as `/a/..//other-host/x`.
