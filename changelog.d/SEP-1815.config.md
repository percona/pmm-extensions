Move HEALTH_REPORT settings out of ExtensionsSettings into a report app-owned settings group: the section now appears under the Health & Security Report group in the admin Settings page and as a top-level `HealthReportSettings` block in the settings export, and is present only when the report app is enabled. The export filter key changed from `ExtensionsSettings.HEALTH_REPORT` to `HealthReportSettings`. Deployed `EXTENSIONS.HEALTH_REPORT` YAML keys, `EXTENSIONS__HEALTH_REPORT__*` environment variables and secret files mounted under those names keep resolving after the move. Beyond the product-wide SEP-to-EXTENSIONS rename (PMM-15570), the move needs no deployment change.
