Retired inventory nodes, services, schemas and tables are now permanently deleted once they have been retired for `EXTENSIONS.INVENTORY.COLLECTION_RETENTION` (30 days by default) and nothing PMM Extensions persisted still resolves them — no backup-catalog run, task envelope, in-flight execution or Celery-beat schedule. Deletion is irreversible and cascades to the entity's subtree and observation rows, so the job stays off unless `EXTENSIONS.INVENTORY.COLLECTION_INTERVAL` is set. The PMM-embedded profile ships it as `1 days`. The schedule is owned by the Inventory plugin and can be toggled from the plugin's task list.
