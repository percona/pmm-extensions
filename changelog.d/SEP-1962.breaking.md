A `DIAGNOSTICS_DELIVERY` plan whose resolution or upload step `path` carries a scheme or host, or whose step `path` of any kind resolves to another host or scheme through its `.` or `..` segments, is now rejected when settings load, so PMM Extensions refuses to start, naming the step kind and its `path` field. A deployment that configured its own plan with such a path has to make the path relative to the plan's `endpoint` before upgrading. A first path segment containing `:`, such as `a:b`, reads as a scheme and is refused too; write it as `./a:b`. The plan baked into the side-car image uses relative paths only and is unaffected.
