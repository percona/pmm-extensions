Operations for MongoDB now collects a bare node's install-readiness facts under the `install_check` scan profile: its operating system, whether it can reach Percona's repository, its package manager, the free space at the data path, and whether a MongoDB process is already running. A node has no service to carry these, so they come from the host's own probe record, letting install readiness be judged from a fresh scan of the target nodes rather than inventory alone.
