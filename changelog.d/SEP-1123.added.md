Declarative `ChainedPredecessor` cascade primitive for plugin schemas: `ChainedPredecessor(name_suffix, on_failure, parent_link)` declared on `PluginSchema.predecessors`, with `cascade_create_predecessors` (all-or-nothing parent-then-predecessors POST + chain wire via `/execute/{first_predecessor_name}` with reverse-order rollback), `cascade_update_predecessors` (best-effort PUT that rejects parent/predecessor renames upfront since the chain wired at create time is not re-fired), and `cascade_delete_predecessors` (best-effort children-first DELETE, 404-tolerant) under `app.extensions.apps.framework.cascade`.
