Added the `NOMAD.finishing_sync_interval_seconds` setting (default `1`), the tick in seconds of the probe that syncs a finished Nomad run ahead of the 30-second sweep, and the deadline of each tick's Nomad listing. It is read when the periodic schedule is seeded at startup, so a change takes effect after a restart, not through a runtime override; set it to `null` to turn the probe off and leave finished runs to the sweep.
