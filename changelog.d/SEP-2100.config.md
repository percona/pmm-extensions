New TASKS__NOMAD__LOG_STREAM_MAX_CONNECTIONS setting (default 64) caps how many Nomad connections the live log viewer's streams hold at once, across every viewer and running task, separately from the tasks service's other Nomad calls. A changed value applies to log streams opened after it lands; streams already open keep their connections until they end. Nomad's limits.http_max_conns_per_client (100 by default) counts every connection from the tasks service's address, these and the other calls together, so keep the setting at 64 or lower unless that Nomad limit has been raised.
