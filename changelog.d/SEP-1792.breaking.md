`percona/pmm-extensions:X` is the frontend-less, app-restricted PMM-embedded image, published from this release on. `percona/percona-sep` keeps the standalone product: every tag published there, `:latest` included, stays standalone, and v0.13.1 is the final standalone release. The `sep_installer.sh` installer and the `-sidecar`/`-embedded` variant tags are retired.
