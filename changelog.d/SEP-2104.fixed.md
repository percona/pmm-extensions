Standalone Tasks and Inventory processes, which the PMM side-car runs as programs of their own, now apply a stored `Settings.LOGGING` override at boot and pick up a change to it within one settings-override refresh interval, without a restart. The standalone Tasks process also applies stored `AlertSettings` overrides to the alerts it raises, and stored `PMM` overrides to its PMM annotations. Both processes read these overrides from the PMM Extensions database and hold one extra connection to it. The side-car already points them at that database; a deployment that runs them against a different one must give them `EXTENSIONS__DATABASE__*`, or they keep their YAML/env values and log a failed refresh every interval. When the PMM Extensions database is unreachable at startup, the override read delays their startup by at most 5 seconds; they then run on their YAML/env values until it answers.
