Operations for MongoDB gains the scan-fact classification that keeps secrets on the host: every fact is public, sensitive, or secret, a secret value or any hash of one is dropped before a fact is ingested (and an unclassified fact is dropped fail-closed), and a config's stored hash is computed over an allowlisted projection rather than the secret-bearing raw file; a member's control state (systemd, supervised, shutdown-only, or unknown) is derived from its unit facts. A scan's classified facts are stored as one current document per subject and profile in `om_observation`, keeping the newer of two results, so a managed-cluster scan's facts never overwrite another profile's and no secret reaches the stored document or its digest. A shell capability step measures what the host actually lets the agent do (`agent.elevation` and the derived unit-control, config-write, package-install and keyFile-read capabilities), replacing the `can_elevate` heuristic that reported yes where nothing elevates and correctly reading a `NoNewPrivileges` unit as unable to elevate. Operations Inventory now also serves the scan surface pmm-managed polls: `POST /api/apps/om_inventory/scans` requests a scan of a managed cluster or an operation's subject, fulfilling once per idempotency key, and `GET /api/apps/om_inventory/observations/{subject_key}` returns a subject's current non-secret facts with the time they were collected, answering 404 for a subject no scan has reported. A requested scan now runs: pmm-managed sends the members its subject resolves to, each with the key its observation lands under and its host, and the scan dispatches to every member's host to capture its systemd and `pbm status` output, storing one observation per member; a scan whose subject resolves to no member finishes with nothing scanned rather than waiting out its lease.
