Operations for MongoDB gains the scan-fact classification that keeps secrets on the host: every fact is public, sensitive, or secret, a secret value or any hash of one is dropped before a fact is ingested (and an unclassified fact is dropped fail-closed), and a config's stored hash is computed over an allowlisted projection rather than the secret-bearing raw file; a member's control state (systemd, supervised, shutdown-only, or unknown) is derived from its unit facts.
