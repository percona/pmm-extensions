Operations for MongoDB gains the scan-fact classification that keeps secrets on the host: every fact is public, sensitive, or secret, a secret value or any hash of one is dropped before a fact is ingested (and an unclassified fact is dropped fail-closed), and a config's stored hash is computed over an allowlisted projection rather than the secret-bearing raw file; a member's control state (systemd, supervised, shutdown-only, or unknown) is derived from its unit facts. A scan's classified facts are stored as one current document per subject and profile in `om_observation`, keeping the newer of two results, so a managed-cluster scan's facts never overwrite another profile's and no secret reaches the stored document or its digest. A shell capability step measures what the host actually lets the agent do (`agent.elevation` and the derived unit-control, config-write, package-install and keyFile-read capabilities), replacing the `can_elevate` heuristic that reported yes where nothing elevates and correctly reading a `NoNewPrivileges` unit as unable to elevate.
