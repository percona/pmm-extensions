Two admins saving related Operations for MongoDB probe-timing settings (POLL_INTERVAL, TASK_TIMEOUT, STALE_RUN_AFTER) at the same moment no longer both get a success while neither change takes effect. The changes are applied one after the other, and the second is refused with the usual 422 if it no longer fits the first. If the other change takes longer than 5 seconds, the request answers 503 and changes nothing.
