PBM Configuration app, served at `/api/apps/backup_mongo/config/` as a child of MongoDB Backups, giving PBM's cluster-wide configuration its own surface alongside Backups and Restores instead of riding along with backup creation. It covers the config file `pbm config --file` writes: storage and its tuning (chunking, retries, server-side encryption, per-node endpoints), point-in-time recovery, backup options, and PBM's `restore` section, which had no representation before. Expert settings sit behind a single "Show advanced options" control. Credentials remain absent by design — access keys, session tokens and the SSE customer key have nowhere safe to live in SEP, so they stay the business of whoever has the `pbm` CLI, and an apply carries them through untouched.
