Setting `AUTH__PROVIDER__CASDOOR__WITHHOLD_BODY_LOG`, the deprecated `CASDOOR__WITHHOLD_BODY_LOG`, or `AUTH__PROVIDER__GRAFANA__WITHHOLD_BODY_LOG` to `false` now stops PMM Extensions at startup with a validation error, because the Casdoor and Grafana clients always keep their request and response bodies out of the log. Remove the setting to start again; `true` is still accepted.
