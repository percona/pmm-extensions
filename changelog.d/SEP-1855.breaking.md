Inventory node and service writes under `/api/inventory/` are now restricted to the internal service principal: `POST /nodes/`, `PUT /nodes/{id}`, `DELETE /nodes/{id}`, `POST /nodes/{id}/revive`, `POST /nodes/{id}/services/`, and the matching `PUT /services/{id}`, `DELETE /services/{id}` and `POST /services/{id}/revive` answer 403 for an **admin** user token. Lower-ranked human tokens were already refused by the app-level role gate, so only admins see a change. Syncer writes are unaffected — they authenticate with `EXTENSIONS_INTERNAL_TOKEN` and are admitted by identity. Reads and lists, the node and service system-observation upserts, and schema and table writes are all unchanged.
