Support diagnostics no longer shows its setup screen to an administrator when diagnostics delivery is already usable, for example when the delivery plan's credentials come from settings.yaml or the environment rather than a DIAGNOSTICS_DELIVERY_INPUTS override saved through Settings. The app now opens whenever the backend reports no reason the send is unavailable; the setup screen still appears when both the backend and the stored settings say delivery is unusable, such as on the PMM side-car image with empty baked credentials. Regular users are unaffected and keep getting the app as before.
