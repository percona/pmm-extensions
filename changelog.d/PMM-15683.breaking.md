Operations for MongoDB now gives each inventory setting a range: `SCHEDULE` from 10 minutes to 1 day, in minutes, hours or days; `TASK_TIMEOUT` from 60 to 300 seconds; `POLL_INTERVAL` from 1 to 30; `REPO_TIMEOUT` from 2 to 30; `CONNECT_TIMEOUT` from 1 to 30; `MAX_CONCURRENT_PROBES` from 1 to 8; `RUN_RETENTION` from 10 to 1000; and `STALE_RUN_AFTER` up to a day. `REPO_TIMEOUT` and `CONNECT_TIMEOUT` together may take at most half of `TASK_TIMEOUT`, and the rule that `POLL_INTERVAL` must not exceed `TASK_TIMEOUT` is dropped because no values within the ranges can break it. A value outside its range, or a `REPO_TIMEOUT` and `CONNECT_TIMEOUT` over half of `TASK_TIMEOUT`, that v3.10.0 accepted under `EXTENSIONS.OM_INVENTORY` in `settings.yaml` or the `EXTENSIONS__OM_INVENTORY__*` environment variables now stops PMM Extensions from starting, for every app and not only Operations for MongoDB, until it is fixed: the startup error names the field and its range, or the fields and the relationship. Check both before upgrading: `REPO_TIMEOUT=30`, `CONNECT_TIMEOUT=30` and `TASK_TIMEOUT=60` are each in range but break the relationship. A PATCH through the admin settings router that sets a value outside its range answers 422. An override stored before the upgrade that falls outside its range is ignored: the field runs on its YAML, environment or default value, and a warning naming it is logged on every settings refresh, so a schedule stored in seconds falls back to the deployment's schedule, 10 minutes by default.
