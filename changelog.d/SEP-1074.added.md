Declarative `DerivedTask` cascade primitive for plugin schemas: `DerivedTask(name_suffix, arg_substitutions, parent_link)` declared on `PluginSchema.derived`, with `cascade_create_tasks` (all-or-nothing POST with reverse-order rollback), `cascade_update_tasks` (best-effort PUT preserving rename-orphan safety), and `cascade_delete_tasks` (best-effort children-first DELETE, 404-tolerant) under `app.extensions.apps.framework.cascade`.
