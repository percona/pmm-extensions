On the PMM-embedded side-car, a host added after the daily host-facts run now gets its first measurement attempt within 15 minutes (the inventory-sync interval) of appearing in the inventory instead of waiting up to a day, so a host that can be measured reports `can_elevate` from then on. A host whose attempt fails is retried at most hourly, three times, and then only by the daily run; each failed attempt raises the standard inventory-sync failure alert. Standalone profiles are unchanged.
