A task's live log opened in the first seconds after it starts now waits for the first step and then streams its output, instead of ending at once with a 410 error or a premature finish. The log stream now answers a run that has not started yet with a retryable 409 `extensions-error` event rather than a 410 or a `finish` carrying a non-terminal status. A run Nomad is holding until the cluster frees capacity is also answered as not started yet instead of as gone, and its log streams when opened after Nomad places it.
