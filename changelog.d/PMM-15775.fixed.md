A restart of PMM Extensions or of PMM Server during an Operations inventory scan no longer blocks every scan for 4 hours: once the interrupted scan has gone 2 minutes without reporting, the next scan records it as failed and runs. That scan no longer picks up the probes the interrupted one left running, so it reports every node instead of failing the ones that scan had in flight. A probe that could not be stopped no longer blocks the node's next scans either.
