Operations Inventory has a new `AUTO_SCAN` setting (`OmInventorySettings.AUTO_SCAN`, on by default): turning it off stops every sweep the app starts on its own, on its schedule and when PMM switches Operations on or restarts, while scans started on request keep running. The switch-on and restart sweeps follow it only with a PMM Server that no longer queues a sweep of its own; an older one still scans every node each time pmm-managed starts.
